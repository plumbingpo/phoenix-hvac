Furnace Repair in Phoenix

Most furnace breakdowns come from a handful of parts: flame sensors, igniters, inducer motors, and control boards. We find the one that failed and fix it, usually in a single visit. Every visit includes a safety check of the burners and venting, and you'll know the price before we start.

  • No ignition when the heat calls
  • Burners light, then shut off within seconds
  • Blower running but the air isn't warm
  • Loud banging, squealing, or grinding at startup

Most air conditioner repairs in Phoenix run between $150 and $650, depending on the part. Common fixes like capacitors, contactors, and fan motors sit at the lower end, while compressor and refrigerant work costs more. If the system is beyond economical repair, full replacement typically ranges from $5,000 to $12,000 installed depending on size and efficiency. We diagnose first and give you a clear, upfront quote before any work begins.
A good rule of thumb: if the system is under 10 years old and the fix is a component, capacitor, motor, igniter, sensor, repair usually makes sense. If the compressor or heat exchanger has failed, the unit is past 12 to 15 years old, or repairs are stacking up season after season, replacement is the smarter investment. Our technicians walk you through both numbers honestly so you can decide with real information.
We offer same-day and emergency HVAC service throughout the Phoenix area.
